What it checks

  • Skill against the simple baseline: the mean, or seasonal naïve
  • Accuracy against always saying the majority class
  • ROC AUC below 0.55: a coin flip
  • Too good: AUC or R² of 0.98 and up, or skill of 95%+
  • MASE of 1 or more: seasonal naïve wins
  • Interval coverage against its target
  • One column carrying the whole model
  • Holdouts under 500 rows
